// About PowerPulse

Two decades keeping Kenya's industry running.

FIG.02 · ON SITE

Nairobi's thermal engineering specialists

PowerPulse is a Nairobi-based thermal engineering company. If it involves heat, steam or combustion, it's our field — and our core work is the supply, installation and ongoing maintenance of industrial boilers, burners, incinerators and related spare parts.

With two decades of hands-on thermal engineering experience across Kenya and East Africa, we're the trusted partner for manufacturing, food processing, healthcare and hospitality operations — and an authorised dealer for leading European boiler and burner brands.
